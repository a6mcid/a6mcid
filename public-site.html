About MCID

Office of Signals and Circulation

The Meme Creature Intelligence Division monitors the emergence, spread, and decline of meme creatures across major social platforms. MCID does not create, promote, or suppress meme creatures; it observes and reports on their activity for the benefit of the general public.

MCID coordinates with interagency liaisons where a creature's spread intersects with matters of general public interest, and maintains the Reference Archive as a historical record of confirmed sightings.

MCID does not have intervention authority. Questions regarding meme creature policy should be directed to the Meme Creature Policy Board (MCPB).
